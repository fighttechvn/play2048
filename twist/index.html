What is Brain Twist?

Brain Twist is a tricky puzzle game built entirely on lateral thinking. Every level poses a tiny riddle, question, or scene — and the obvious answer is always a trap. To win you have to break the rules the puzzle quietly assumes: tilt your phone, drag one object onto another, tap something hidden off-screen, or reread the question until the trick jumps out.

There's no math to grind and no reflexes to master. Just you, a curious little scene, and that delicious "ohhh, that's sneaky" moment when the real answer finally clicks. It's the classic brain-teaser format — reinvented with playful animations, warm art, and a difficulty curve that eases you in before it starts pulling the rug.
