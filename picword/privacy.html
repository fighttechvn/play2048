Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

PicWord ("the app", "we") is a 4 Pics 1 Word puzzle game published by FightTech. This policy explains what data the app stores on your device and the limited data it sends to our servers to run online features. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress is stored locally on your device and is not, by itself, sent to us:

  • Level progress, coins, owned items, daily-bonus date
  • Settings such as language, sound on/off and theme

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this local data.

2. Data we send to run online features

To provide leaderboards and seasonal events, the app may send the following to our backend (gobrain.fighttech.vn):

  • A random guest identifier / token generated on your device. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Gameplay results for leaderboards — e.g. the level solved and how long it took.
  • Basic technical data needed to serve requests, such as app language and device platform.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not request your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone/camera access to play.

3. Optional analytics, ads and purchases

Depending on the build and your region, the app may use:

  • Analytics (e.g. Google Firebase / Google Analytics) to understand aggregate, anonymous usage such as which levels are played. This can be used to improve the game.
  • Rewarded ads (e.g. Google AdMob), if enabled — you choose to watch an ad to earn coins. Ad networks may process device identifiers under their own privacy policies.
  • In-app purchases handled by Google Play Billing / Apple. Payment details are processed by the store, not by us.

If these features are not enabled in the version you have installed, no such data is collected by them.

4. How we use data

We use the data above only to run and improve the game — powering leaderboards and events, keeping the service reliable, and understanding aggregate usage. We do not sell your data.

5. Data sharing

We share data only with the service providers that make these features work (hosting, analytics, ad and payment providers listed above), and where required by law. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

6. Data retention

Leaderboard and event data associated with your guest identifier is retained while the service operates. You can request deletion (see Contact) or by clearing app data / uninstalling for the on-device portion.

7. Children

PicWord is a family-friendly puzzle game and contains no objectionable content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where a child-directed experience applies, ads and analytics are configured accordingly.

8. Your choices

  • Play offline: most of the game works without any network connection.
  • Clear or delete local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.
  • Request deletion of server-side leaderboard data linked to your guest identifier via the contact below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
