Domino Fit · Level 1Covered 0/6
How to play. Drag dominoes from the tray to cover every cell. Each colored region has a rule: a number means the pips must add up to it, = means all pips equal, <N means the sum stays under N, ≠ means all different. Tap a domino to rotate it; drag a placed one off the board to remove it.
