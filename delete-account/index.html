Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Go Brain account and associated data. Go Brain is developed by FightTech (app package vn.fighttech.go2048). The only account Go Brain has is an anonymous guest profile you create when you join the online leaderboard.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Go Brain on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your leaderboard profile, display name, and scores are removed from our server, and your local game data is cleared.

Option 2 — Request by email (e.g. after uninstalling)

If you have already uninstalled the app, email us and we will delete your data for you:

  • Email: fighttech.vn@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my Go Brain data
  • Include the display name you used on the leaderboard so we can find your profile.

We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.
